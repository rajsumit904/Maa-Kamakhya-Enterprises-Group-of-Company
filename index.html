Who We Are

Based in Mathurapur, Bhagalpur, Bihar, Maa Kamakhya Enterprises is a project-focused group of companies engaged in Civil and Mechanical Works, Construction Consulting, Contract Coordination, Manpower Support, and Material Supply. We aim to build long-term business relationships with EPC contractors, project developers, industrial organizations, and infrastructure companies across India.

Our Core Services

Civil Engineering & Construction

Industrial and commercial civil works, building construction, structural works, and civil maintenance.

Mechanical Works

Mechanical installation, structural steel fabrication, industrial maintenance, and erection support.

Earthwork & Machinery

Excavation, Soil grading, Site leveling, and Provision of heavy construction machinery.

Project Consulting & Coordination

Tender participation, BOQ review, rate analysis, commercial proposal preparation, and work-order coordination.

Manpower & Material Supply

Provision of skilled, semi-skilled, and unskilled labor, along with high-quality construction material procurement.
